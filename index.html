The person behind it

I paint every set by hand, in a spare bedroom studio.

I started making press-ons for friends during a slow year in nursing school and never really stopped. These days I keep every order to a few sets a week and paint each design freehand rather than printing it — so no two sets are ever quite identical, even in the same style.

Don't see a color or shape you're after? Send a reference photo. Most custom sets ship within two days.

How ordering works

From your inbox to your fingertips, in four steps.

1

Pick or request a design

Choose a set from the gallery, or send a reference photo for a custom quote.

2

Send your measurements

A quick sizing guide gets emailed to you so the set fits without any filing.

3

I paint your set

4

It ships to your door
